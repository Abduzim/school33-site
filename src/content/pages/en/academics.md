The school provides general secondary education under the national education standards and curriculum of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

## Grades

- **Primary** — grades 1–4
- **Middle** — grades 5–9
- **Senior** — grades 10–11

The school has 20 classes, and all of them study in the first shift.

## Electronic gradebook

Grades, attendance and homework are recorded on [emaktab.uz](https://emaktab.uz). Ask the class teacher for your login and password.
