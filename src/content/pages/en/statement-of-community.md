---
title: "Our values"
description: "The values shared by students, parents and staff of School No. 33."
---

Our school is a shared home for students, parents and teachers. We hold to these values:

- **Respect** — we value every person’s views and dignity.
- **Honesty** — in our studies and in how we treat each other.
- **Love of learning** — we help every student reach their potential.
- **Safety** — school must be a safe place for everyone.
- **Partnership** — families and the school achieve most when they work together.
