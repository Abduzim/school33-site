---
title: "Child protection"
description: "How the school keeps children safe and how to raise a concern."
---

Every student should feel safe at school. Our staff do not tolerate violence, discrimination or humiliation of children.

## If you are worried

If you think a child is at risk, tell the class teacher or the school office straight away: [+998 99 899 87 81](tel:+998998998781). In an emergency, call 102 (police) or 103 (ambulance).

## What the school does

- Takes every report seriously and keeps it confidential.
- Informs parents and the relevant authorities when needed.
- Holds regular child-safety briefings with staff.
