## Grade 1 admission

Children who turn 7 in the year of admission start grade 1. Applications are made online at [my.maktab.uz](https://my.maktab.uz) or [my.gov.uz](https://my.gov.uz). You can apply to only one school at a time.

### Dates

For the 2026–2027 school year, admission ran as follows:

- **20 June – 31 July** — for families living in the school’s catchment area;
- **1–31 August** — applications from outside the catchment area.

Dates for the next school year are announced by the Ministry of Preschool and School Education. This page will be updated once they are published.

### How to apply

1. Sign in to [my.gov.uz](https://my.gov.uz) with OneID, or open [my.maktab.uz](https://my.maktab.uz).
2. Choose the service for enrolling a child in grade 1.
3. Enter the child’s and parent’s details and choose School No. 33, Qibray District.
4. Track the status of your application in your personal account.

## Transferring from another school

Transfers from another school are also made through [my.maktab.uz](https://my.maktab.uz). Call the school first to check that the class has a free place.
